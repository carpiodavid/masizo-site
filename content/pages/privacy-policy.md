# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: [LAUNCH DATE — set at publication]. Last revised: October 23, 2026.**

This policy explains what information **Masizo Development LLC** ("Masizo," "we," "us") collects when you use this website or communicate with us by phone, text message, or email — and what we do with it. We've written it in plain language on purpose: you should be able to read your contractor's privacy policy without hiring a translator.

## The Short Version

We collect what you give us (your name, contact details, and project information) plus standard website analytics. We use it to respond to you, run your project, and understand how the site is used. We don't sell it, and we never share your mobile number or text-messaging consent with anyone for marketing.

## Information We Collect

**Information you provide.** When you call, text, email, or submit our contact form, we receive what you send: typically your name, phone number, email address, city, the service you're interested in, and whatever you tell us about your project. As a job progresses, project records (estimates, scope notes, scheduling details, photos of the work) accumulate the same way.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, we use analytics to understand how visitors use the site — pages viewed, approximate location (city level), device and browser type, and how you arrived here. We use **Google Analytics 4** for this; it sets cookies and collects usage data under [Google's own privacy terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy). If we later run advertising campaigns, a Google Ads tag or Meta (Facebook) pixel may also be active; when one is, it measures ad performance (for example, that a visit or form submission followed an ad click) and this policy covers it. You can limit cookies through your browser settings, and analytics works fine without identifying you personally to us — we see patterns, not names.

**Our hosting provider.** This site is served by **Cloudflare**, which processes technical request data (such as IP addresses) as part of delivering and securing the site.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

- Respond to your inquiry and schedule consultations and estimates;
- Provide and manage construction, remodeling, and restoration services — including project updates, scheduling, and invoicing;
- Answer questions, provide support, and act on feedback;
- Understand how the website performs so we can improve it; and
- Comply with legal, licensing, and insurance obligations.

## What Happens to a Form Submission

When you submit our contact form, your submission is stored in a secure datastore operated by Cloudflare (Workers KV) so it can't be lost in transit, and a copy is emailed to our office through **Resend**, an email delivery service. Both act as service providers processing your information only to deliver your message to us.

## Text Messaging (SMS) Program

If you provide your phone number — through our form, by calling, or in person — and agree to receive texts, Masizo Development LLC may send you text messages about your inquiry, project updates, consultation scheduling, and service alerts. Here is the whole deal, which also appears in our [Terms and Conditions](/terms-and-conditions):

- **Consent:** Providing your number and opting in means you agree to receive these messages. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any service.
- **Frequency:** Varies with your project's status.
- **Cost:** Message and data rates may apply, per your carrier plan.
- **Opt out:** Reply **STOP** at any time and we'll stop texting you (you'll receive one confirmation message).
- **Help:** Reply **HELP** or email [[EMAIL]].
- **Age:** You must be 18 or older to opt in.
- **No sharing:** We do not share your mobile phone number, text-messaging originator opt-in data, or consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Ever.

## What We Don't Do With Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only with the service providers named above (analytics, hosting, datastore, and email delivery — each processing it to provide their service to us), with parties involved in your project when you ask us to (for example, your insurance company on a restoration claim), or when the law requires it.

## Security and Retention

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold, and we keep it only as long as we need it — to serve you, maintain required business and licensing records, and meet legal obligations. No website or system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise the impossible; we can promise we treat your information like we'd want ours treated.

## Your Choices and Rights

You can ask us at any time to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it (subject to records we're legally required to keep). You can withdraw SMS consent by replying STOP, unsubscribe from any marketing email via its unsubscribe link, and limit analytics cookies in your browser. To exercise any of these, use the contact details below — we don't make you file paperwork to be forgotten.

## Changes to This Policy
