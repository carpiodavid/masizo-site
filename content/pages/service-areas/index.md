Masizo Development is a Gilbert-based general contractor, and our working map is the one a Gilbert contractor can honestly serve: the East Valley around us, the Phoenix metro core, and the communities northeast of it — [[CITIES]]. We've built in Arizona for [[YEARS]] under state licenses ([[LICENSE_GC]], [[LICENSE_RESTORATION]], plus [[LICENSE_CA]] in California) that apply across every city below; what differs from one city to the next is the local permitting office, and navigating each one is part of what you hire us for. Every service we offer — [custom homes, ADUs, remodeling, additions, roofing, restoration, and commercial work](/services) — is available throughout this footprint.

One honesty note before the list: we publish a dedicated page for a city only when we can show completed Masizo projects there, with real photos. Cities below without their own page aren't served any less — they're simply waiting on us to document the local work properly rather than fake it.

## Cities We Serve

### Gilbert

Home base. Our office is here, which makes Gilbert the shortest drive on our map for everything from a kitchen remodel to a 2 a.m. water emergency. Gilbert is also covered by Arizona's statewide ADU law — our [ADU law guide](/guides/arizona-adu-law) explains what that opens up for single-family lots like the ones across town.

### Mesa

One of the largest cities in Arizona, with housing that spans a century — which means Mesa work runs the whole menu, from updating mid-century and 1980s-era homes to additions and roofing on newer builds. Mesa is among the cities covered by the state ADU law ([guide](/guides/arizona-adu-law)).

### Chandler

Chandler's neighborhoods are dominated by subdivisions now reaching the age where original kitchens, baths, and roofs come due all at once — prime territory for remodels and whole-home updates. It's also an ADU-law-covered city ([see the guide](/guides/arizona-adu-law)).

### Queen Creek

Queen Creek brings us larger lots and land on the growing edge of the Valley, where a parcel may need a well, a septic system, or new utility runs before a [custom home](/services/custom-homes) can rise on it. Evaluating exactly that is a standard part of our lot consultations out here.

### San Tan Valley

San Tan Valley projects often run through county rather than city permitting, and rural-style utilities are common — a different paperwork path and site checklist than the incorporated East Valley, and one we work regularly. If you're buying land here, talk to us before you close.

### Tempe

Tempe is essentially built out, so adding space means working with the lot you have: [room additions](/services/room-additions), remodels of older housing stock, and ADUs — Tempe is covered by the state ADU law ([guide](/guides/arizona-adu-law)), which makes a backyard unit one of the few ways to add a dwelling in a landlocked city.

### Scottsdale

Scottsdale work carries a higher design bar, and we like that. Remodels and additions here need to meet the finish level of the surrounding home and neighborhood. Scottsdale is also an ADU-law-covered city ([details in the guide](/guides/arizona-adu-law)).

### Phoenix

The biggest market on our map and the widest range of housing eras — from historic-district bungalows to brand-new builds on the fringes. Phoenix is covered by the state ADU law ([guide](/guides/arizona-adu-law)), and ADU and remodel demand here reflects it.

### Fountain Hills

Northeast of Scottsdale, Fountain Hills means hillside lots, desert views, and homes where the site itself — slopes, access, drainage — shapes the project as much as the floor plan does. We bring the same crews and licensing up the Beeline as we do across the East Valley.

### Paradise Valley

Paradise Valley is a town of large custom residences, and projects here are judged against that standard: quality of finish, discretion on site, and respect for architecture that was expensive to get right the first time.
